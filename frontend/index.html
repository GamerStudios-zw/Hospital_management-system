Hospital Departments

Specialized modules for every department

  • Reception
  • Nursing
  • Medical
  • Pharmacy
  • Inventory

Reception Department

First point of contact for patients

The reception module handles patient registration, appointment scheduling, queue management, and vital initial screening. Staff can quickly register new patients, verify information, and route them to appropriate departments.

Nursing Department

Patient care and vital monitoring

Nurses have access to patient records, can record vital signs, manage admissions, monitor patient progress, and coordinate care with doctors. The system supports shift roster management and ward monitoring.

Medical (Doctor) Department

Clinical consultation and treatment

Doctors can view patient history, conduct consultations, write digital prescriptions, order tests, and maintain treatment records. The system provides comprehensive patient information for informed clinical decisions.

Pharmacy Department

Medicine dispensing and inventory

The pharmacy module manages medicine stock, processes prescriptions, tracks dispensing, monitors inventory levels, and generates reorder requests. Integration with doctor prescriptions ensures accurate dispensing.

Inventory Department

Hospital-wide resource management

Comprehensive inventory management for all hospital supplies beyond pharmacy. Track equipment, consumables, and other resources with automated alerts for low stock and reorder management.
