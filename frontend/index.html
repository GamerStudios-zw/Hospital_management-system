How Care Moves Through HMS

A simple operating sequence that keeps each role focused while preserving data integrity.

01

Capture

Patient profile, identity, and coverage are validated at intake before queue placement.

02

Triage

Vitals and notes determine urgency routing to nurse in charge stream without manual ambiguity.

03

Treat

Consultation and prescriptions are recorded with smart prompts and status-safe actions.

04

Dispense + Close

Pharmacy verifies fulfillment, then dashboards update live for end-to-end traceability.

Security and Compliance by Design

Critical safeguards are built into workflow behavior, not added as afterthoughts.

  • Strict role-based authorization on backend routes
  • Heavy field validation on client and server inputs
  • Appointment state locks prevent unsafe edits
  • Session timeout handling with controlled logout flow
  • Consistent audit-oriented actions across dashboards
